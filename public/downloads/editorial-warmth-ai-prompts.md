# AI Prompt Templates: Editorial Warmth

## Palette
- Apricot Whisper Soft: #F5F1EA
- Amber Silk Soft: #C9C092
- Citrine Tone Muted: #ABAB87
- Garnet Velvet Soft: #90475F
- Olive Dusk Muted: #5C6647

## Mood Keywords
paper grain, warm margin, publisher desk, amber ink, essay

## Suggested Use Cases
Publishing sites, Blogs, Narrative landing pages

## Prompt 1: Visual Design

# Color Psychology: Candy Pop

Bright, playful, and deliberately high-energy. Built for maximum visual impact on social media, e-commerce surfaces, and campaign landing pages.

---

## Coral Radiant Vivid (#E18A33)
**Family:** Orange · **Lightness:** 54% · **Saturation:** 74%
**Mood:** Warmth, enthusiasm, creativity
**Associations:** Friendliness, confidence, adventure. Orange balances energy with approachability.
**Caution:** Can feel cheap or overly casual without supporting neutrals.

## Citrine Tone Vivid (#E4E44E)
**Family:** Yellow · **Lightness:** 60% · **Saturation:** 74%
**Mood:** Optimism, clarity, warmth
**Associations:** Happiness, intellect, caution. Yellow is the most visible color and suggests openness.
**Caution:** Too much yellow can strain eyes; use sparingly as accents.

## Mint Core Clear (#38BC4E)
**Family:** Green · **Lightness:** 48% · **Saturation:** 54%
**Mood:** Balance, harmony, growth
**Associations:** Nature, health, prosperity, stability. Green is the easiest color for eyes to process.
**Caution:** Murky greens can suggest decay; keep saturation intentional.

## Peony Core Vivid (#D520B7)
**Family:** Pink · **Lightness:** 48% · **Saturation:** 74%
**Mood:** Expression, beauty, tenderness
**Associations:** Playfulness, romance, softness. Modern pinks range from bold fuchsia to quiet blush.
**Caution:** Stereotypical use feels dated; pair with unexpected neutrals.

## Azure Bloom Clear (#A1CDE3)
**Family:** Blue · **Lightness:** 76% · **Saturation:** 54%
**Mood:** Trust, depth, intelligence
**Associations:** Professionalism, security, calm. Blue is the most universally preferred color.
**Caution:** Overuse creates coldness; warm it with complementary tones.

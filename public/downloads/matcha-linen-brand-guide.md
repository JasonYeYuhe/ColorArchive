# Brand Usage Guide: Matcha & Linen

A calm, crafted palette pairing soft matcha greens with warm paper tones. Designed for wellness products, tea packaging, and artisan brand surfaces that need organic warmth.

---

## Color Roles

### Slot 1: Primary (backgrounds, hero surfaces)
**Moss Silk Soft** · #A4C992

### Slot 2: Secondary (UI panels, borders)
**Leaf Bloom Muted** · #BACDB7

### Slot 3: Supporting neutral (body text areas)
**Olive Pearl Muted** · #D9DECF

### Slot 4: Accent (CTAs, buttons, highlights)
**Apricot Veil Muted** · #FBFAF9

### Slot 5: Deep contrast (headings, anchoring elements)
**Honey Whisper Soft** · #F3F5EA

## Application Guidelines

### Do
- Use **Moss Silk Soft** as the dominant surface color (60% of the layout)
- Use **Leaf Bloom Muted** for secondary panels and content areas (30%)
- Reserve **Apricot Veil Muted** for interactive elements and calls to action (10%)
- Pair **Honey Whisper Soft** with light backgrounds for readable body text

### Don't
- Don't use the accent color (**Apricot Veil Muted**) for large background areas
- Don't combine more than 2 saturated colors in the same section
- Don't use light palette colors for body text on white backgrounds — check contrast first

## Suggested Pairings
- **Hero section:** Moss Silk Soft background + Honey Whisper Soft text + Apricot Veil Muted CTA button
- **Card layout:** Leaf Bloom Muted card background + Honey Whisper Soft heading + Olive Pearl Muted border
- **Dark mode:** Invert Honey Whisper Soft as background, Moss Silk Soft as text

# Brand Usage Guide: Forest Terrain

A palette rooted in organic outdoor materials — bark, moss, amber soil, and limestone. Use it when the brand needs to feel grounded, natural, and tactile.

---

## Color Roles

### Slot 1: Primary (backgrounds, hero surfaces)
**Moss Tone Muted** · #93AB87

### Slot 2: Secondary (UI panels, borders)
**Leaf Dusk Soft** · #437439

### Slot 3: Supporting neutral (body text areas)
**Olive Silk Muted** · #B2BC9F

### Slot 4: Accent (CTAs, buttons, highlights)
**Amber Velvet Soft** · #908347

### Slot 5: Deep contrast (headings, anchoring elements)
**Honey Shadow Muted** · #50543B

## Application Guidelines

### Do
- Use **Moss Tone Muted** as the dominant surface color (60% of the layout)
- Use **Leaf Dusk Soft** for secondary panels and content areas (30%)
- Reserve **Amber Velvet Soft** for interactive elements and calls to action (10%)
- Pair **Honey Shadow Muted** with light backgrounds for readable body text

### Don't
- Don't use the accent color (**Amber Velvet Soft**) for large background areas
- Don't combine more than 2 saturated colors in the same section
- Don't use light palette colors for body text on white backgrounds — check contrast first

## Suggested Pairings
- **Hero section:** Moss Tone Muted background + Honey Shadow Muted text + Amber Velvet Soft CTA button
- **Card layout:** Leaf Dusk Soft card background + Honey Shadow Muted heading + Olive Silk Muted border
- **Dark mode:** Invert Honey Shadow Muted as background, Moss Tone Muted as text

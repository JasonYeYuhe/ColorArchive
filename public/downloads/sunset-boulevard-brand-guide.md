# Brand Usage Guide: Sunset Boulevard

A gradient palette that moves from coral glow through amber warmth to rose-tinged dusk. Built for travel, lifestyle brands, and any surface that needs golden-hour energy.

---

## Color Roles

### Slot 1: Primary (backgrounds, hero surfaces)
**Coral Bloom Clear** · #E3C2A1

### Slot 2: Secondary (UI panels, borders)
**Amber Silk Clear** · #D9CB81

### Slot 3: Supporting neutral (body text areas)
**Ruby Radiant Soft** · #B26F62

### Slot 4: Accent (CTAs, buttons, highlights)
**Rose Pearl Soft** · #E4C8DB

### Slot 5: Deep contrast (headings, anchoring elements)
**Garnet Tone Clear** · #D06287

## Application Guidelines

### Do
- Use **Coral Bloom Clear** as the dominant surface color (60% of the layout)
- Use **Amber Silk Clear** for secondary panels and content areas (30%)
- Reserve **Rose Pearl Soft** for interactive elements and calls to action (10%)
- Pair **Garnet Tone Clear** with light backgrounds for readable body text

### Don't
- Don't use the accent color (**Rose Pearl Soft**) for large background areas
- Don't combine more than 2 saturated colors in the same section
- Don't use light palette colors for body text on white backgrounds — check contrast first

## Suggested Pairings
- **Hero section:** Coral Bloom Clear background + Garnet Tone Clear text + Rose Pearl Soft CTA button
- **Card layout:** Amber Silk Clear card background + Garnet Tone Clear heading + Ruby Radiant Soft border
- **Dark mode:** Invert Garnet Tone Clear as background, Coral Bloom Clear as text

# Brand Usage Guide: Terracotta Loft

A palette drawn from kiln-fired materials — terracotta, warm stucco, dried rust, and bleached linen.

---

## Color Roles

### Slot 1: Primary (backgrounds, hero surfaces)
**Coral Velvet Soft** · #906B47

### Slot 2: Secondary (UI panels, borders)
**Ember Dusk Muted** · #665147

### Slot 3: Supporting neutral (body text areas)
**Ruby Shadow Muted** · #543F3B

### Slot 4: Accent (CTAs, buttons, highlights)
**Amber Tone Soft** · #BCB076

### Slot 5: Deep contrast (headings, anchoring elements)
**Honey Silk Muted** · #B7BC9F

## Application Guidelines

### Do
- Use **Coral Velvet Soft** as the dominant surface color (60% of the layout)
- Use **Ember Dusk Muted** for secondary panels and content areas (30%)
- Reserve **Amber Tone Soft** for interactive elements and calls to action (10%)
- Pair **Honey Silk Muted** with light backgrounds for readable body text

### Don't
- Don't use the accent color (**Amber Tone Soft**) for large background areas
- Don't combine more than 2 saturated colors in the same section
- Don't use light palette colors for body text on white backgrounds — check contrast first

## Suggested Pairings
- **Hero section:** Coral Velvet Soft background + Honey Silk Muted text + Amber Tone Soft CTA button
- **Card layout:** Ember Dusk Muted card background + Honey Silk Muted heading + Ruby Shadow Muted border
- **Dark mode:** Invert Honey Silk Muted as background, Coral Velvet Soft as text

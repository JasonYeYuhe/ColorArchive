# Brand Usage Guide: Ocean Abyss

A palette built around the pressure and light of deep water — dark teal bases, cobalt mid-tones, and vivid aqua accents.

---

## Color Roles

### Slot 1: Primary (backgrounds, hero surfaces)
**Teal Shadow Clear** · #216E54

### Slot 2: Secondary (UI panels, borders)
**Aqua Dusk Soft** · #397474

### Slot 3: Supporting neutral (body text areas)
**Cerulean Nocturne Soft** · #223F44

### Slot 4: Accent (CTAs, buttons, highlights)
**Lagoon Silk Vivid** · #71EAD6

### Slot 5: Deep contrast (headings, anchoring elements)
**Cobalt Velvet Clear** · #3158A5

## Application Guidelines

### Do
- Use **Teal Shadow Clear** as the dominant surface color (60% of the layout)
- Use **Aqua Dusk Soft** for secondary panels and content areas (30%)
- Reserve **Lagoon Silk Vivid** for interactive elements and calls to action (10%)
- Pair **Cobalt Velvet Clear** with light backgrounds for readable body text

### Don't
- Don't use the accent color (**Lagoon Silk Vivid**) for large background areas
- Don't combine more than 2 saturated colors in the same section
- Don't use light palette colors for body text on white backgrounds — check contrast first

## Suggested Pairings
- **Hero section:** Teal Shadow Clear background + Cobalt Velvet Clear text + Lagoon Silk Vivid CTA button
- **Card layout:** Aqua Dusk Soft card background + Cobalt Velvet Clear heading + Cerulean Nocturne Soft border
- **Dark mode:** Invert Cobalt Velvet Clear as background, Teal Shadow Clear as text

# Color Psychology: Sunset Boulevard

A gradient palette that moves from coral glow through amber warmth to rose-tinged dusk. Built for travel, lifestyle brands, and any surface that needs golden-hour energy.

---

## Coral Bloom Clear (#E3C2A1)
**Family:** Orange · **Lightness:** 76% · **Saturation:** 54%
**Mood:** Warmth, enthusiasm, creativity
**Associations:** Friendliness, confidence, adventure. Orange balances energy with approachability.
**Caution:** Can feel cheap or overly casual without supporting neutrals.

## Amber Silk Clear (#D9CB81)
**Family:** Yellow · **Lightness:** 68% · **Saturation:** 54%
**Mood:** Optimism, clarity, warmth
**Associations:** Happiness, intellect, caution. Yellow is the most visible color and suggests openness.
**Caution:** Too much yellow can strain eyes; use sparingly as accents.

## Ruby Radiant Soft (#B26F62)
**Family:** Red · **Lightness:** 54% · **Saturation:** 34%
**Mood:** Energy, urgency, passion
**Associations:** Power, love, danger, excitement. Red draws attention and creates a sense of immediacy.
**Caution:** Overuse can feel aggressive or alarming.

## Rose Pearl Soft (#E4C8DB)
**Family:** Pink · **Lightness:** 84% · **Saturation:** 34%
**Mood:** Expression, beauty, tenderness
**Associations:** Playfulness, romance, softness. Modern pinks range from bold fuchsia to quiet blush.

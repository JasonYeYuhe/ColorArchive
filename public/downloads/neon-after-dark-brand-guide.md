# Brand Usage Guide: Neon After Dark

Electric contrast between deep nocturne bases and vivid neon accents. Built for gaming interfaces, nightlife branding, and any product that needs to glow in the dark.

---

## Color Roles

### Slot 1: Primary (backgrounds, hero surfaces)
**Fuchsia Radiant Vivid** · #E133E1

### Slot 2: Secondary (UI panels, borders)
**Aqua Bloom Vivid** · #95EFEF

### Slot 3: Supporting neutral (body text areas)
**Lime Bloom Clear** · #C2E3A1

### Slot 4: Accent (CTAs, buttons, highlights)
**Violet Nocturne Clear** · #21174F

### Slot 5: Deep contrast (headings, anchoring elements)
**Cobalt Ink Soft** · #182030

## Application Guidelines

### Do
- Use **Fuchsia Radiant Vivid** as the dominant surface color (60% of the layout)
- Use **Aqua Bloom Vivid** for secondary panels and content areas (30%)
- Reserve **Violet Nocturne Clear** for interactive elements and calls to action (10%)
- Pair **Cobalt Ink Soft** with light backgrounds for readable body text

### Don't
- Don't use the accent color (**Violet Nocturne Clear**) for large background areas
- Don't combine more than 2 saturated colors in the same section
- Don't use light palette colors for body text on white backgrounds — check contrast first

## Suggested Pairings
- **Hero section:** Fuchsia Radiant Vivid background + Cobalt Ink Soft text + Violet Nocturne Clear CTA button
- **Card layout:** Aqua Bloom Vivid card background + Cobalt Ink Soft heading + Lime Bloom Clear border
- **Dark mode:** Invert Cobalt Ink Soft as background, Fuchsia Radiant Vivid as text

# Color Psychology: Neon After Dark

Electric contrast between deep nocturne bases and vivid neon accents. Built for gaming interfaces, nightlife branding, and any product that needs to glow in the dark.

---

## Fuchsia Radiant Vivid (#E133E1)
**Family:** Pink · **Lightness:** 54% · **Saturation:** 74%
**Mood:** Expression, beauty, tenderness
**Associations:** Playfulness, romance, softness. Modern pinks range from bold fuchsia to quiet blush.
**Caution:** Stereotypical use feels dated; pair with unexpected neutrals.

## Aqua Bloom Vivid (#95EFEF)
**Family:** Teal · **Lightness:** 76% · **Saturation:** 74%
**Mood:** Calm, sophistication, clarity
**Associations:** Trust, serenity, emotional balance. Teal combines blue's stability with green's freshness.
**Caution:** Very dark teals can feel heavy without light counterpoints.

## Lime Bloom Clear (#C2E3A1)
**Family:** Lime · **Lightness:** 76% · **Saturation:** 54%
**Mood:** Freshness, growth, vitality
**Associations:** Nature, renewal, youth. Lime bridges the energy of yellow with the calm of green.
**Caution:** Can feel acidic or jarring in large areas.

## Violet Nocturne Clear (#21174F)
**Family:** Purple · **Lightness:** 20% · **Saturation:** 54%
**Mood:** Creativity, luxury, mystery
**Associations:** Royalty, wisdom, imagination. Purple suggests premium quality and artistic sensibility.
**Caution:** Dark purples can feel heavy; balance with lighter values.

## Cobalt Ink Soft (#182030)
**Family:** Blue · **Lightness:** 14% · **Saturation:** 34%
**Mood:** Trust, depth, intelligence
**Associations:** Professionalism, security, calm. Blue is the most universally preferred color.
**Caution:** Overuse creates coldness; warm it with complementary tones.

1. **SaaS subscription** — recurring billing with predictable revenue, not one-time impulse purchases
2. **No downloadable files** — all features are in-app, nothing to dispute as "not received"
3. **Free tier available** — users can fully evaluate before subscribing, reducing buyer's remorse
4. **Higher price point** — $6.99–$99.99 range, well above the low-value threshold
5. **Instant activation** — subscription unlocks features immediately, zero fulfillment delay
6. **7-day refund policy** — transparent refund policy reduces disputes

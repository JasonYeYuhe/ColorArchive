## Submission Copy

### Short (60 chars)
Browse, search & explore 3,066 curated hex colors for design

### Tagline (120 chars)
A calm, searchable library of 3,066 colors with palette tools, Figma tokens, and Tailwind exports — all free, no signup.

### Description (500 chars)
ColorArchive is a curated library of 3,066 algorithmically generated colors organized by hue, lightness, and saturation. Browse the full spectrum, build custom palettes, extract colors from images, and generate brand palettes with AI. Export to CSS, Figma tokens, Tailwind config, SwiftUI, and more. Includes 290+ design guides, 170+ curated collections, and free downloadable palette packs. Built for designers, developers, and brand teams who need production-ready color values without the noise.
